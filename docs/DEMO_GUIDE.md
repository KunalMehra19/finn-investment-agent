# Two-minute FINN walkthrough

1. Open the app. The briefing starts with Current / 7 days, separate from Historical replay. Notice the completed EOD session date (13 November 2026 in the initial seed), 15-price coverage, and filing snapshot date.
2. Open L&T’s Noida Metro story. The order classification is a ₹1,000–2,500 crore band, **not** an exact price. Open the original NSE attachment at PDF page 2. Inspect the 15.1 km, 10 stations and 36-month scope in the original.
3. Investigate L&T for the current order analysis, or select HDFC Bank / TCS from the research desk for richer financial context. Ask “What changed in margins?” and open the cited evidence. Without a Gemini key, the UI explicitly labels the deterministic editorial retrieval.
4. Open the Promise ledger. TCS’s directional margin statement is compared with its later adjusted margin. Sun Pharma’s R&D spend is below the original range; its qualitative growth target stays Unclear. Each comparison has separate promise and actual actions.
5. Open Watchlist. Change daily move or relative-volume thresholds, switch following on/off, and return to Current to see updated EOD crossings. Preferences and saved stories persist on this browser.
6. Switch to Current / 24h. The initial retrieved period has no material filings within that window; FINN says so. Switch to Historical replay to inspect the five curated results events without changing their original dates.
7. Search HCLTECH, ASIANPAINT or AXISBANK. A successful Yahoo lookup shows verified identity and EOD data. Document research is explicitly unavailable; no invented financial answers appear. A nonexistent ticker returns a calm validation error.
8. Open the evidence library from the desktop rail. Original documents open on issuer/NSE domains. Mobile users can also reach every company source through Research → Source documents.
